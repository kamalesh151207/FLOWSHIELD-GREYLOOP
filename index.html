FLOODSHIELD AUTOMATED BYPASS MECHANISM

Hydraulic overflow prevention and groundwater protection

The Conventional Soak-Pit Overflow Problem

Conventional rural soak pits rely strictly on continuous soil percolation. When monsoonal downpours occur, soil saturation prevents absorption. The pits fill and overflow into village lanes, forming stagnant pools that breed disease vectors.

The FLOWSHIELD Solution

FLOWSHIELD deploys a capacitive moisture probe at the infiltration zone. As saturation exceeds 85%, the controller actuates a motorized valve to isolate the recharge bed, diverting treated effluent safely into a raised bypass channel.

SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS & SIH PROBLEM STATEMENT

PROJECT: FLOWSHIELD–GREYLOOP

Flood-Resilient, Minimum-Footprint Modular Greywater Management System

PROBLEM STATEMENT: SIH26257

Decentralized greywater treatment and management system designed for rural households and land-constrained communities.

CORE INNOVATION

Combines a compact multi-layer vertical biofilter (biochar, coco coir, sugarcane bagasse, rice husk) with automated soil-moisture-based recharge control and FloodShield bypass protection to prevent soak-pit failure.

● DEMONSTRATION SIMULATION MODE

This dashboard interface is operating in Demonstration Simulation Mode for the Smart India Hackathon jury presentation.

All telemetry values (flow rate, water levels, soil moisture, and water quality benchmarks) are dynamically computed by the prototype simulation service.
